Hypsognattus, the spelling in the request, remains an alias for Hypsognathus. Coelacanths are represented by the existing Latimeria genus and Latimeriidae family under Coelacanthiformes. Both chart searches find Latimeria for coelacanth.

The chart retains its broad, conventional Parareptilia group and Pareiasauridae family usage, rather than restructuring existing reptile lineages. Some papers use Elginiidae as a more narrowly defined family for Elginia. Dilophosaurus is placed directly under Theropoda instead of creating a disputed Dilophosauridae family: Marsh and Rowe did not recover that family as monophyletic. Supporting ranks follow the user’s request to show families where supported; no unrelated taxa were added.

All new nodes are extinct and use their rank fills with red borders and daggers. The entry-content configuration, IndexedDB schema and existing scientific-name storage keys are preserved; image browsing changes only the displayed image index.

## v5.9 batch — Livyatan, Therizinosaurus and webspinners

- **Livyatan → Odontoceti**: the fossil genus is a stem physeteroid. It sits directly under the broader toothed-whale group, beside the existing Physeteridae branch, rather than forcing it into the disputed content of that family or inventing an extinct family. [Lambert et al., macroraptorial sperm whales](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/zoj.12456). Existing whale ancestry chains are untouched.
- **Therizinosaurus → Therizinosauridae → Maniraptora**: its own extinct family uses the same rank styling as other theropod families. [Natural History Museum classification](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/dino-directory/therizinosaurus.html).
- **Embia → Embiidae → Embioptera → Insecta**: living webspinners are insects, with both the order and a representative genus/family available in search. [Royal Entomological Society](https://www.royensoc.co.uk/understanding-insects/classification-of-insects/embioptera/), [Zoological Survey of India checklist](https://www.zsi.gov.in/uploads/documents/importantLinks/english/33.pdf). Aliases include webspinner, web spinner and their plurals.

Three requested animal entries/groups, six total chart nodes, 997 taxa / 996 links. The webspinner follow-up belongs to the same ongoing batch and does not trigger an extra release. v5.9 counter is 0/5. No existing IDs/titles or editor storage format changed.
